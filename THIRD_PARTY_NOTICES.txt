MoonAlign 1.1.1 — third-party components

The portable Windows application includes these components:
Python 3.10.11 and its standard library (including tkinter)
Tcl/Tk 8.6 (the graphical interface runtime)
NumPy 2.2.6 (including its wheel's bundled numerical libraries)
OpenCV / opencv-python 4.13.0.92 (including its wheel's bundled libraries)
Pillow 12.1.1 (including its wheel's bundled image libraries)
PyInstaller 6.22.3 bootloader
typing_extensions 4.15.0 (included for NumPy's compatibility imports)

Full license texts and bundled third-party notices copied from the installed
distributions are included under licenses/. Those texts, including notices
for bundled numerical and image-codec libraries, govern their components.
PyInstaller's license includes the bootloader exception; see its COPYING.txt.
The application source is licensed separately in LICENSE.

No photographic source images are included in this distribution.
MoonAlign processes local files and does not upload images.
